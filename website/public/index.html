Who is Connect for?

Connect is for adults 18+ who want to explore intentional connections. Your profile and preferences help describe the kind of connection you want.

Can I use Connect in my browser?

Yes. Create an account or sign in to manage your profile, discover people, browse matches, chat when eligible, join activities and manage your settings.

How do conversations start?

Mutual interest creates a match. Conversation access follows the match’s unlock requirements, including any configured quest or review. Paying doesn’t bypass consent or safety requirements.

What does a verification badge mean?

A badge describes a specific account signal or review status. It does not guarantee personal safety, identity in every context, or future behavior. Visit the safety center for the available controls.

Are paid plans and live calls available?

Hosted checkout is available when billing is configured, and active calls open a provider room shared by both participants. This local build uses provider test settings; production billing and a private call host remain separate release gates.
