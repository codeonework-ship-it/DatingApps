Community guidelines

Bring curiosity.
Leave room for respect.

Good connections start with the way we treat one another.

Be yourself, honestly.

Use accurate profile information and photos you have permission to share. Don’t impersonate someone else, misrepresent your age or use another person’s identity.

Respect a no, a pause or a goodbye.

Consent is ongoing. A match, gift or shared activity is never an obligation to reply, meet, continue a conversation or share personal information.

Keep this space free of harm.

Don’t threaten, harass, discriminate, scam, pressure or distribute someone’s private information or intimate content without consent. Don’t bypass blocks or account restrictions.

Use the tools when something feels wrong.

Report concerning behavior from the relevant profile or conversation. Block members when you need a boundary. Follow an available appeal through your account if you disagree with a moderation decision.
